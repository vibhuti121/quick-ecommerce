1 · The one-paragraph summary

We have a working online store where a shopper can land, browse products, add them to a cart, and check out — with the order confirmed and saved reliably. It is built the way large retailers build (many small, independent services rather than one big program), so it can grow without being rebuilt. The shopping experience works today. What stands between it and real customers is the operational layer — a real payment company, a live website address, polish and safety nets (Section 5). Think: the engine, chassis and a test-drive-ready body are built and verified — it drives — but it isn't yet registered, insured or given its final paint.
